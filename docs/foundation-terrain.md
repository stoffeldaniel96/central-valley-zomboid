# Level foundation constraints and Viewpoint interoperability
Decision: every ordinary building foundation must be planar in the sanitized terrain, including exterior threshold access and adjoining hardscape as appropriate. Split-level structures/stepped foundations can use explicitly separate level pads; do not warp walls to terrain. Keep absolute DEM immutable. Determine pads before final terrain fitting, after footprint reconciliation, and revalidate after structural snapping. Related surfaces, stairs, entrances, drainage, rail, ramps and bridge clearances must be solved jointly. No per-building arbitrary offsets or independent nearest-pad blending in production. Flat pad may be positive or negative relative to shared game baseline. Flag unresolved competing constraints.

## Precedents (reviewed 2026-11-26)
- Better Terrain: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3815223174. Author describes level foundations, mild town grades, physical terrain using Java/game physics, support for vehicles, players and zombies. Published target B42.21; standard map data only, multiplayer not validated. Changelog mentions vehicle contact at chunk edges, aim, corpse placement and lighting fixes. No source algorithm or custom-data ingestion contract verified.
- Rolling Hills: https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3815630947. Describes flat major towns, level rural foundations, water and highway protection; visual elevation distinct from underlying collision, separate physics addon. Published 0.2.31 target B42.22/Viewpoint 0.1.7. Not a physical terrain solution by itself.
- Viewpoint API: https://docs.projectviewpoint.org/api/view-state/ documents terrainHeight(x,y) read access to rendered relief, x east/y south in squares and z in storeys, storey scale sqrt(6) square widths. This is not a setter, and does not establish native collision or heightfield import. Internal Relief class listed as red in https://docs.projectviewpoint.org/reference/viewpoint-internals/; avoid depending on unstable internals. Ask upstream for custom map heightfield/provider contract, flat foundation masking, handling native slopes without double elevation, negative Z and shared physics in isometric/3D. Do not assume runtime dependency, reuse rights or current compatibility. Version-pin local inspection/testing.
