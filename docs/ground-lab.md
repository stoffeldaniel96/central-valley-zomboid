# Ground-first acceptance gate
Before buildings, prove the ground plane. Author signed continuous terrain Z in meters, with one persistent regional reference in a verified vertical datum. Do not derive per-site offsets, clamp negative Z or assume sea level equals game Z0. No regional reference elevation chosen yet. Engine Z/storey scale is a separate adapter calibration. A height field is only a single ground sheet; bridge decks, tunnels, buried spaces and engineered surfaces are separate volumes/sheets with topology and clearance, not DEM edits.

## First laboratory
Run py src/ground_lab.py --fixture. Synthetic 64m square with diagonal grade and recessed channel, positive and negative elevations. Generates continuous shared-vertex OBJ (east/north/up, meters, for inspection only), numeric NPZ and JSON quantitative comparison. No missing-data extrapolation. Sample 16 points per native square and compare flat proxy and provisional eight-direction slope proxy. Defaults 2.5% grade increments, 5cm center increments, 3m experimental storey; not actual PZ parameters. Cross-storey tiles and edge seam disagreements explicitly flagged. Slope family counts are geometric proxies, not final sprite counts. Rare families review at <10 placements. Do not hide cracks via artwork or claim these are supported native slopes.
