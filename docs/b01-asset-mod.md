### Installed engine bytecode diagnosis — 42.22.2
Read-only inspection of user-supplied engine_inspection.zip SHA256 a622337cedd2e60272275d76ced294064592b3349eec609c508db6bbff21a30d; IsoGridSquare.class SHA256 b8cef32a10bb76433bad174397b51f1a046be1eef47997dc1e79dd8547d726c5. javap disassembly successful. The direction switch mapping contains all eight directions; getSlopedSurfaceHeight(float,float) only handles N/S/W/E and returns zero for remaining directions. Local inputs are clamped to 0..1; integer min/max are divided by 100 after interpolation. getApparentZ(float,float) delegates to this height method for non-stairs and adds square Z. The direction-argument overload returns max for uphill, min for opposite, -1 otherwise, or zero for no slope. isSlopedSurfaceEdgeBlocked compares same-level adjacent opposite edge scalar by exact equality (no slope returns unblocked, missing adjacent blocks for sloped source); CalculateCollide checks both sides for cardinal neighbors, with level-above exceptions. Consequently identical -1 sentinels may accept different edge profiles, and equal-height fractional floors may be blocked on a perpendicular edge because of the sentinel. connectedWithFloor and hasSlopedSurfaceToLevelAbove use the edge overload for storey transitions; testCollideSpecialObjects and CalculateCollide call level-above logic. Do not globally replace the sentinel overload with midpoint height or bypass CalculateCollide (would affect unrelated walls, water, stairs, etc.).
